ABOUT HIM

Who is Michael Phelps?

A swimmer who changed Olympic history

Michael Fred Phelps II was born on June 30, 1985, in Baltimore, Maryland, USA. He started swimming at a young age and quickly showed exceptional talent in the sport.

Phelps made his Olympic debut at the 2000 Sydney Games when he was only 15 years old. Over the following years, he became one of the most successful swimmers in history.

Across five Olympic Games, he won 28 medals: 23 gold, 3 silver and 2 bronze. His achievements have made him an inspiration for athletes around the world.

SUCCESS STORY

23 Olympic Gold Medals

Built through hard work

Michael Phelps' success was not achieved overnight. Years of training, discipline and determination helped him reach the highest level of swimming.

His greatest performance came at the 2008 Beijing Olympics, where he won eight gold medals in a single Olympic Games.

At the 2016 Rio Olympics, Phelps added five more gold medals and completed his Olympic career with a record-breaking 23 gold medals.
