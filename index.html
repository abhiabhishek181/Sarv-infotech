THE JOURNEY

From Young Swimmer to Olympic Legend

A Dream Bigger Than the Pool

Michael Phelps was born on June 30, 1985, in Baltimore, Maryland. He started swimming at a young age and quickly showed exceptional talent in the pool.

At just 15 years old, Phelps competed in his first Olympic Games at Sydney 2000. Although he did not win a medal, the experience became the beginning of an extraordinary Olympic journey.

His breakthrough came at the 2004 Athens Olympics, where he won six gold medals and two bronze medals. Four years later in Beijing, he achieved something historic by winning eight gold medals at a single Olympic Games.

Phelps continued competing at the highest level through London 2012 and Rio 2016. At Rio, he added five more gold medals and finished his career with an incredible total of 28 Olympic medals, including 23 gold.
