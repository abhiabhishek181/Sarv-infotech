ABOUT HIM

Who is Michael Phelps?

A swimmer who changed Olympic history

Michael Fred Phelps II was born on June 30, 1985, in Baltimore, Maryland. He started swimming when he was young and quickly developed exceptional talent.

Phelps made his Olympic debut at the 2000 Sydney Olympics when he was only 15 years old.

During his career, he won 28 Olympic medals, including an incredible 23 gold medals. His success came from years of training, discipline and a strong desire to improve.

"You can't put a limit on anything. The more you dream, the farther you get."

— Michael Phelps

HIS SUCCESS STORY

Talent + Hard Work + Discipline

Michael Phelps' success was not achieved overnight. He spent countless hours training in the pool and preparing himself mentally and physically for competition.

His career teaches us that natural talent is only one part of success. Discipline, consistency, patience and the ability to overcome challenges are equally important.

From a teenager making his Olympic debut to becoming the most decorated Olympian in history, Phelps created a legacy that continues to inspire athletes worldwide.
